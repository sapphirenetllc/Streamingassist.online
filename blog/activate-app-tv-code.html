TV showing an activation code? Do this.

That 6-letter code on your TV screen is completely normal — it's how apps link your TV to your account. But here's what most people get wrong: they enter it on the wrong page, and the activation silently fails. Our experts handle this exact call dozens of times a day. Here's the safe way.

The safe 3-step code entry

  • Step 1 — Leave the code on your TV screen. Don't press anything; codes expire if you navigate away.
  • Step 2 — On your phone or computer, type the activation address shown on the TV manually. Don't tap a search result — look-alike pages harvest logins.
  • Step 3 — Enter the code exactly, sign in, then watch the TV confirm. Done — your app library should load within a minute.

"Code expired" or "invalid"?

Go back on the TV to generate a fresh code, then enter it within a few minutes. Old screenshots and yesterday's codes never work — always use the code currently on screen.

Stuck in a login loop?

If entering the code just returns you to the sign-in screen, the usual culprits are: an app that needs updating, a subscription tied to a different email, or corrupted app data. Update the app first; if the loop persists, remove and reinstall it, then try the code flow once more.
