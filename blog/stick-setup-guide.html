Setup guide • 6 min read • Updated Oct 2026

New streaming stick? Set it up in 20 minutes.

Unboxed a plug-in streaming stick and staring at cables? Take a breath. The whole job is about 20 minutes if you do things in the right order: power, network, account, apps. Here's exactly how our experts do it on every call.

Step 1: Plug in power properly

Connect the stick to an HDMI port, then plug its power cable into a wall outlet, not the TV's USB port, which often underpowers it and causes random restarts. Switch the TV to the matching HDMI input.

Step 2: Pair the remote first

Put fresh batteries in and hold the remote close to the stick during pairing. If it won't pair, unplug the stick for 30 seconds and try once more before anything else. 90% pair on the second attempt.

Step 3: Join the fast Wi-Fi band

Connect to your router's 5 GHz network (not the 2.4 GHz one) and install any software update it offers. Updates during setup prevent a whole category of weird app bugs later.

Step 4: Sign in and enter the TV code carefully

You'll get an on-screen code to enter on an activation page. Type the page address yourself rather than clicking search results. Look-alike pages are the #1 trap. Enter the code, sign in or create your account, and wait for the TV to confirm.

Step 5: Install only the apps you'll use

Add your movie, sports and live TV apps one by one and sign into each. Installing everything at once eats storage and slows budget sticks to a crawl.

The 3 mistakes behind most callbacks

  • Powering the stick from the TV's USB port instead of the wall
  • Joining the slow 2.4 GHz network during setup
  • Entering the activation code on a look-alike site from search results
